# news-image-20261122

One image summing up the world's news of Sunday **22 November 2026**: Iran
striking a US air base in Kuwait with Bahrain intercepting projectiles, 128
nations agreeing at the UN to draft an international instrument on lethal
autonomous weapons ("killer robots"), the International Court of Justice ruling
Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the FBI probing a breach of
its National Crime Information Center, the Nasdaq sinking 5.28% in a $6
trillion AI wipeout, and COP31 climate talks running into overtime in deadlock
on fossil fuels.

`main.py` writes `entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, 117 KB) to `/output/entry.png`.
Rendered by the entering agent from that day's reporting (news.com.au, SBS,
Business Standard, NPR, Times of India).
